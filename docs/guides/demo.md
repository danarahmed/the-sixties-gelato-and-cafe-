# Showing the system to another café (the practice café as the demo)

When you show the system to someone thinking of using it, show it in **practice**. Practice is the same app, but with pretend money and pretend stock, in a practice café of your own. Nothing you do there reaches The Sixty's books, its stock or its reports.

## Before the visit (5 minutes)

1. Sign in as yourself, open **Practice** and press **Start** (or **Start again** for a fresh café, as on the first day).
2. The practice café is the same type as yours, with the same parts on (`0120`). To show a restaurant or a supplier, ask the system's administrators for a demo business of that type, or turn the parts on in your practice café's Settings.
3. Sell a few things at the till, so the dashboard and the reports have something to show.

## What to show, in this order

1. **The till:** a quick sale paid by card, a sale paid in cash with the change, and a table's bill kept open and paid later.
2. **The menu on a phone** (`0118`): turn it on in Settings, open its link on their phone, choose two things and show the waiter's scan at the till.
3. **The end of the day:** close the drawer, counted blind, and look at what it should have held.
4. **Stock:** a delivery received, a count, and what was lost.
5. **The books:** Reports → the day's profit, "Do the books tie?", and Against last year and the budget.
6. **Languages:** switch to Kurdish or Arabic at the top; everything changes, the right way round.

## Afterwards

- Leave practice with the button at the top: the screen goes back to the real café.
- Practice → **Start again** clears the pretend café for next time; your ticks stay.

Nothing shown in practice can be mistaken for the real café: every screen says **Practice** at the top while it is on.
