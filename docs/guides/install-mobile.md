# Install on iPhone / iPad / Android

## iPhone & iPad (Safari)

1. Open **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome) and go to your app address.
2. Sign in once.
3. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up arrow).
4. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Name it **Sixty's** and tap **Add**.
6. Launch it from the Home Screen — it runs full-screen like a native app and
   works offline.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open **Chrome** and go to your app address.
2. Sign in once.
3. Tap the menu (⋮) → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Confirm. Launch **Sixty's** from your home screen / app drawer.

## Tips

- Large tap targets and a bottom-reachable layout make one-handed use easy.
- Arabic and Kurdish switch the whole layout to right-to-left.
- The online/offline indicator shows whether sales are syncing live or queued.
