# Owner Operating Guide

Welcome. This system is your single source of truth. You do not need to be
technical to use it. Daily work is visual and hard to misuse.

## The big idea

- **Stock counts itself.** You never type a "current stock" number. The system
  adds up every receipt, sale, production, waste, and transfer to know exactly
  what you have — down to a single straw.
- **Numbers can't be quietly changed.** Sales and stock moves are permanent;
  fixing a mistake creates a visible correction, so the history is always honest.
- **Profit is shown honestly** — several numbers, not one, so you see the truth
  after discounts, platform commissions, and costs.

## Your dashboard

Today's net sales, gross profit, contribution profit, orders and average order
value, cash/card/platform split, expected platform payouts, low-stock and
expiring items, waste, variances, best/worst sellers, and alerts that need you.

## What only you (and managers) can do

- See costs, profit, and platform contracts.
- Approve stock adjustments, waste, refunds, and discounts over the set limits.
- Lock/reopen accounting periods (accountant/owner).
- Turn AI on/off and approve its suggestions.

## Weekly rhythm (suggested)

1. Review the dashboard alerts each morning.
2. Approve pending adjustments/waste with a reason.
3. Check Talabat reconciliation after each settlement — chase any flagged
   missing payout or wrong fee.
4. Do a cycle count of high-value items.
5. Review best/worst sellers and channel margins.

## Golden rules

- Enter waste when it happens — it keeps stock and cost accurate.
- Use the right channel at POS (dine-in vs takeaway vs Talabat) so packaging is
  deducted correctly.
- Replace the demo prices/costs with your real figures before going live.
